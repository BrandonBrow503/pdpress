Rules of thumb

In the US in 2026, books published in 1930 or earlier are free to use. On January 1, 2027, books from 1931 join them. That covers the original text and art as first published.

Still protected: later revised editions, newer translations, forewords and notes added later, film versions, and trademarks such as series names and character brands.

Amazon turns down public-domain books that look like the free copies. A real annotated or illustrated edition, with your added work clearly described, is what gets approved.
